# 🎓 BufiB Kinderakademie

### A digital learning platform for children to discover Islam in an engaging, structured and age-appropriate way.

**BufiB Kinderakademie** is an online learning platform created for children who want to learn more about Islam in a clear, interactive and child-friendly environment.

The platform combines live online lessons with digital learning materials, quizzes and progress tracking. Children learn step by step, while parents can follow their learning progress and access lesson materials.

---

## ✨ What the platform offers

- 👧🧒 Learning groups for different age ranges
- 💻 Weekly online lessons via Zoom
- 📚 Structured Islamic learning content
- 📖 Quran learning and understanding
- 🧠 Interactive quizzes
- 🏅 Badges and learning motivation
- 📝 Digital lesson materials
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent access to progress and learning content
- 🎯 Age-appropriate and easy-to-follow lessons

---

## 📚 Learning Areas

Children explore different areas of Islamic knowledge, including:

- Faith & Foundations
- Ahlulbayt & Islamic History
- Quran
- Islamic Values & Character
- Everyday Islamic Practice

---

## 🎯 The Goal

The goal of **BufiB Kinderakademie** is to make Islamic education more accessible, engaging and motivating for children.

Instead of simply consuming content, children actively learn through lessons, exercises, quizzes and digital learning materials.

The platform is designed to create a positive learning experience that connects Islamic knowledge with modern digital education.
